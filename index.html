Key Policy Timeline Events
Jan 2021
Biden EO 13985 — "Advancing Racial Equity." Federal DEI programs expanded across all agencies. All 50 states benefit from federal DEI support.
Jun 2023
SCOTUS SFFA v. Harvard — Race-conscious admissions declared unconstitutional. Triggers first wave of state anti-DEI legislation.
2023–2024
FL, TX, NC, ND, ID, IN, AL, UT, IA, KS, TN, WY pass state-level DEI bans or restrictions at public universities.
Jan 20, 2025
Trump EO 14151 — "Ending Radical Government DEI Programs." Terminates all federal DEI offices, positions, and mandates on Day 1.
Jan 21, 2025
Trump EO 14173 — Revokes LBJ's 1965 EO 11246 on affirmative action. Targets private sector DEI. Asks AG to identify "most egregious DEI practitioners."
Feb 13, 2025
16 blue-state AGs (CA, NY, IL, MA, MD, WA, OR, CO, MN, MI, PA, VT, NJ, CT, RI, DE) issue joint guidance affirming DEI legality.
Feb 2025
19 red-state AGs (TX, AL, AR, GA, ID, IA, KS, KY, LA, MO, MT, NE, ND, OH, OK, SC, SD, TN, VA) join anti-DEI enforcement coalition.
2025
New state bans enacted in AR, KY, MS, OK, WV, AZ. Multiple federal lawsuits challenge Trump EOs. Federal court in MD temporarily blocks enforcement.
Apr 2025
Trump Education Dept. demands states certify "no illegal DEI." 21 states comply; CA, MA, MN, NY, PA, VT, WA refuse.
Feb 18, 2026
Federal judge invalidates anti-DEI university guidance. Trump admin drops appeal. But 22+ states still have active DEI bans in law.

Key Policy Events
Jan 20, 2021
Biden EO 13985 — Advancing Racial Equity
Federal DEI programs expanded across all agencies. All 50 states benefit from federal DEI support and funding mandates.
Jun 29, 2023
SCOTUS SFFA v. Harvard & UNC
Supreme Court rules race-conscious admissions unconstitutional 6–3. Triggers the first wave of state anti-DEI legislation.
2023–2024
First & Second Wave State Bans
FL (SB 266), TX (SB 17), NC (SB 364), ND (HB 1446), ID (HB 710), IN (HB 1003), AL (SB 129), UT (HB 261), IA (SF 2435), TN (SB 2756), WY (HB 147) pass DEI restrictions at public universities.
Jan 20, 2025
Trump EO 14151 — Ending Radical Government DEI
Day 1: All federal DEI offices, positions, and mandates terminated. Federal contractors required to certify no DEI programs.
Jan 21, 2025
Trump EO 14173 — Ending Illegal Discrimination
Revokes LBJ's 1965 EO 11246 on affirmative action. Targets private sector DEI. AG directed to identify "most egregious DEI practitioners."
Feb 13, 2025
16 Blue-State AGs Defend DEI
CA, NY, IL, MA, MD, WA, OR, CO, MN, MI, PA, VT, NJ, CT, RI, DE issue joint guidance reaffirming DEI programs are legal under state and federal law.
Feb 2025
19 Red-State AGs Join Anti-DEI Coalition
TX, AL, AR, GA, ID, IA, KS, KY, LA, MO, MT, NE, ND, OH, OK, SC, SD, TN, VA join enforcement coalition targeting private sector DEI programs.
2025
New State Bans: AR, KY, MS, OK, WV, AZ
SB 3 (AR), HB 4 (KY), HB 1193 (MS), SB 796 (OK), SB 474 (WV), SB 1694 (AZ) extend the legislative ban wave into 2025.
Apr 2025
Trump Education Dept. DEI Certification Demand
States required to certify "no illegal DEI" to receive federal education funding. CA, MA, MN, NY, PA, VT, WA refuse to comply.
Feb 2026
Federal Court Blocks Anti-DEI Guidance
Federal judge invalidates Trump admin's anti-DEI university guidance. Administration drops appeal. However, 22+ states still have active DEI bans encoded in state law.
